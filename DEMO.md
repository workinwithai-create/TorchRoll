# TorchRoll demo note
- **Set up:** plug in a MIDI keyboard and open https://torchroll.vercel.app in Chrome or Edge. Tap to start audio and pick your keyboard under MIDI in. Use BPM 68, key A minor, chords `Am9 Dm9 Gsus4 Cmaj7 Fmaj7 Dm7 E7 Am9`.
- **Play:** press ▶ Play. Hold rootless voicings with your right hand around middle C (C E G B for Am9, F A C E for Dm9…). Torch Roll rolls the chord on 1, answers in triplets and drops a left-hand low note on 3. Ride the **mod wheel** (tremolo) up on the long chords, sweep **CC74** (brightness) darker for the verse and brighter into the turnaround, push **expression/CC11** to let the breath pad swell. Hold **sustain** and switch to Slow Burn for the last two bars.
- **Suggested patch: "Last Call":**
  - Voice Velvet Tine, brightness 45%, attack 4 ms, release 350 ms
  - Recipe Torch Roll, ring 2.5 triplets, roll spread 45 ms, tremolo 35%, breath 55%
  - Drums Brush ballad; mix: keys 0.85, pad 0.55, bass 0.75, brushes 0.6
  - Bars 1–4 Torch Roll, bars 5–6 Lean Back (grace-note leans), bars 7–8 Slow Burn.
- **Record:** press ● Rec, play 2 passes, press Rec again to keep it, then Export WAV + MIDI and drop both on bar 1.
