TORCHROLL

A playable sultry-ballad keyboard for your MIDI keys. Hold a chord and the roll engine comps it in a slow 12/8 lilt (rolled chords, triplet arps, grace-note leans, tempo-locked tremolo) over a breath pad, an upright-style bass and brushes, on the grid of your BPM and key.

Tap to start audio.

1 · Your loop

Your BPM, key and chords are saved in this browser and used for playback and export. Changing the key transposes the chords and your take. The feel is triplet (12/8 inside each 4/4 bar), so exports still sit on a plain 4/4 DAW grid.

2 · Play

bar – · beat –

Your MIDI keyboard plays the keys voice right away and never starts the transport. Stopped, you hear the raw EP so you can audition voicings. Playing, the roll engine comps whatever you hold (or your take, or the chord of the bar). Fallback keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; (Z / X = octave).

3 · Roll engine

12 triplet steps per bar. Codes: R rolled chord · A arp tone · B block chord · L left-hand low note · G grace-note lean · H hold · - rest. Click a step to cycle.

5 · MIDI + audio

Controls: notes + velocity · CC64 sustain · CC1 mod wheel = tremolo depth · CC74 = brightness · CC11 expression / CC2 breath = pad breath · pitch bend ±2 · CC123 all notes off. MIDI out mirrors what you play and the roll engine (ch 1), the bass (ch 3) and brushes (ch 10). No audio input is used.

6 · Export (drops on bar 1 of your DAW)

48 kHz / 24-bit stereo, exactly your bars at your BPM, downbeat at sample 0, ring-out and room tail folded into the start so it loops, peak ≤ −1 dBFS. MIDI: tempo, 4/4, key, one track per chair (Torch Keys, Breath Pad, Velvet Bass, Brush Kit).

Every sound in TorchRoll is synthesized in your browser and named as a synth. No samples, no accounts, no tracking. © 2026 WorkinWithAI.
